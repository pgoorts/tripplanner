TripPlanner Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

TripPlanner ("the app") is a private app used to plan and organize trips, events, notes, and reminders. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is stored, and who it is shared with. TripPlanner is currently made available only to a small, invited group of testers and is not publicly available.

Information We Collect

  • Google account information. TripPlanner uses Google Sign-In to let you log in. We receive basic profile information from your Google account, such as your name, email address, and profile picture, in order to identify you within the app.
  • Trip content you create. This includes trips, events, notes, and reminders you create or that are shared with you, along with associated details such as dates, locations, and descriptions you choose to enter.

How Information Is Stored

Your account information and trip content are stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase is a cloud service operated by Google; data is transmitted and stored securely using Google's infrastructure.

How Information Is Shared

Your trip content is private by default and is only visible to you. If you choose to share a trip with another person, that person will be able to view (and, depending on the permissions you grant, edit) the trip content you shared. We do not sell your information, and we do not share your information with advertisers or other third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the email address below and we will remove it.

Children's Privacy

TripPlanner is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age required to hold a Google account in their country of residence.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact: tripplanner@patrikgoorts.com
